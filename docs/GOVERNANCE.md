# Governance Guidance

This repository makes a prediction model inspectable and reusable. It does not
make the model deployment-ready.

## Minimum Conditions For Applied Use

Before any organization uses this model outside research or sandbox testing, it
should have:

- A clearly documented support-oriented purpose.
- No use for hiring, firing, discipline, promotion, compensation, insurance,
  policing, or access decisions.
- Explicit consent or another ethically and legally valid basis for processing
  sensitive mental-health-related data.
- Privacy review covering data minimization, retention, access control, and
  audit logs.
- Local validation on the intended population.
- Calibration and threshold review before any flagging workflow.
- Subgroup performance assessment.
- Clinician, occupational-health, or qualified mental-health oversight.
- A non-punitive pathway to support.
- A human review step before any outreach or intervention.
- A documented appeal, correction, or opt-out pathway where applicable.

## Prohibited Or High-Risk Uses

Do not use this model to:

- Diagnose suicidal ideation.
- Predict imminent suicide attempt or death.
- Score people without a legitimate support purpose.
- Trigger automated adverse decisions.
- Rank employees, applicants, students, patients, customers, or platform users.
- Replace clinical assessment, emergency response, or direct human care.

## Practical Framing

The safest framing is not "the model identifies suicidal employees." The safer
and more accurate framing is:

> This reference model provides one inspectable signal that can be locally
> validated as part of a broader, consent-based, support-oriented mental-health
> governance workflow.
